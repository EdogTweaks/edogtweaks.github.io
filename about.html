Why I started this

I hate lagging in endgames. You make it through the whole game, the lobby gets small, everybody's building at once, and that's when your game starts to stutter. It's the worst possible time for it.

When that happens, you're not playing at your full potential, and you know it. You can see the play you should've made. Your PC just didn't keep up.

So I started building the tools I wanted for my own game. Edog Tweaks goes after the stuff in Windows that holds your game back, and the macros and crosshair help with the rest of how you play.

I'll be straight with you: Windows shows a warning the first time you open my apps, because they aren't signed yet. So I made a whole Safe to Run page that shows what they do on your PC and how to check the files yourself. If I were the one buying, that's what I'd want to see.

Where the extra FPS comes from

Windows isn't set up for gaming out of the box. It's saving power, running stuff in the background, animating menus and indexing your files, and all of that uses up what your game could be using. Edog Tweaks lets you turn it down.

  • Power plansSwitch to High or Ultimate Performance so your PC stops holding back to save power.
  • Visual effectsTurn off the animations and see-through windows that don't help you win anything.
  • Background workStop things like Superfetch and search indexing from running while you play.
  • Services and bloatTurn off services you don't use and remove apps that came with Windows, with a keep list for the important ones.
  • Input delayChange settings like core parking and USB power saving, so your game can feel more responsive even when FPS doesn't move.

So how much FPS will you get?

It depends on your PC, and I won't make up a number. A bloated Windows install has the most to gain. A PC that's already tuned has less. The app shows your live CPU, GPU and RAM use so you can see what changed, and if you don't like a tweak, you can switch it back.
