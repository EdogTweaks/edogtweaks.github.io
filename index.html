Questions

Everything people ask before they buy.

Can I get banned for using these?

Edog Tweaks only changes Windows settings, and Edog Crosshair draws on top of your screen. Neither one reads or changes the game.

Edog Macro and Edog Controller Macro press keys and buttons for you. Many games and most tournaments don't allow macros, so check the rules for wherever you play. You use them at your own risk.

Where do I get my access code?

It shows up on the Payhip page right after you pay, next to your download, and Payhip emails it to you too. It looks like EDMC-7KQ2X-9XHTP-L3MW8-ZD4FQ.

Lost it? Open the Payhip email again, or post in the Discord's support channel with your order email and we'll resend it. The downloads are pinned in the Discord as well.

Do I have to enter the code every time?

No. You paste it once, the first time the app opens on your PC, and it stays unlocked after that, even offline. A bundle code unlocks all four apps. Your code is for you, so please don't share it.

Will the macro work with my keyboard and mouse?

Yes. Edog Macro works with any keyboard and mouse that Windows sees. You pick the Edit, Select and Place keys in the app, so it matches whatever binds you already use.

Which controllers does Edog Controller Macro support?

Xbox controllers, PS4 DualShock 4 and PS5 DualSense, over USB or Bluetooth, on a Windows PC. It uses the free ViGEmBus driver, and the installer offers to set it up if you don't have it yet. It doesn't run on a console.

Will Edog Tweaks mess up my PC?

Every tweak saves the setting it replaces, so you can switch it back off and your old value returns. The app makes a Windows restore point before your first change and has an Undo everything button. It never turns off Windows Defender or installs drivers.

Does the crosshair show up in fullscreen?

Use Windowed Fullscreen or Borderless in your game settings. Exclusive fullscreen doesn't let any overlay draw on top. You can also hide the crosshair from recordings and streams in Settings.

How much FPS will I gain?

It depends on your PC. A fresh or bloated Windows install gains the most. A PC that's already tuned gains less. The dashboard shows your live CPU, GPU and RAM use so you can see the difference yourself.

What if an app doesn't work on my PC?

Post in the Discord's support channel with your Windows version and what happened, and we'll help you get it running.
